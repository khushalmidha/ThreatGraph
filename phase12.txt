## PHASE 12 — Containment Simulation



**Goal:** Safe, simulated enforcement layer — never touches anything real.



**Do:**

1. `simulation/vlan/`, `simulation/acl/`, `simulation/policies/`: zone model (USER/SERVER/DATABASE/SECURITY/QUARANTINE) with example ACL matrix from the spec.

2. Automatic mode (CRITICAL risk → simulated isolation policy) and manual analyst-approval mode. Full workflow: Detection → Incident → Risk Escalation → Recommended Action → Analyst Approval → Simulated Isolation → Verification → Rollback.

3. `POST /containment/isolate/{host_id}`, `POST /containment/release/{host_id}` — every action logged with host, policy, reason, timestamp, evidence, and rollback capability, persisted to `containment_actions` and `audit_logs`.

4. `GET /policies`, `POST /policies`, `DELETE /policies/{id}` for the simulated network policies.



**Acceptance checks:**

- Isolating a host visibly changes simulated traffic behavior afterward (e.g., the generator respects the simulated policy and blocked flows show up as denied, not silently dropped from the demo).

- Rollback (`/containment/release`) actually restores prior simulated policy state.
