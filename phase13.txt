## PHASE 13 — Authentication, RBAC, Audit



**Goal:** Security-as-first-class-concern, not an afterthought.



**Do:**

1. JWT auth, password hashing, roles (ADMIN/ANALYST/VIEWER). Only ADMIN/ANALYST can call containment endpoints.

2. Every sensitive action (containment, policy change) generates an audit log entry.

3. Input validation, rate limiting, CORS config, safe error handling (no stack traces leaking to clients), no secrets in the repo — confirm `.env.example` stays accurate.

4. Authorization tests: VIEWER blocked from containment, ADMIN/ANALYST allowed, audit log populated on every attempt (including denied ones).



**Acceptance checks:**

- A VIEWER-role JWT gets a 403 on `POST /containment/isolate/{id}`; the attempt still appears in `audit_logs`.



**Commit checkpoint:** `phase 13 — auth, RBAC, audit logging`
