## PHASE 8 — Risk Engine



**Goal:** Turn model outputs into an explainable 0–100 risk score.



**Do:**

1. `backend/app/risk/`: configurable risk computation combining graph threat probability, transformer score, anomaly score, historical host risk, rule evidence, asset criticality, attack-path evidence.

2. Severity bands (0–24 NORMAL, 25–49 LOW, 50–74 HIGH, 75–100 CRITICAL) — configurable, not hard-coded magic numbers scattered through code.

3. Every risk score must carry an evidence list (no unexplained scores) — persisted to `risk_scores`.

4. `GET /hosts/{id}/risk` implemented for real.



**Acceptance checks:**

- Feeding a Phase 2 lateral-movement scenario through Phases 3–7 produces a HIGH/CRITICAL score with a populated, non-empty evidence list.

- Feeding normal traffic produces a NORMAL/LOW score.



**Commit checkpoint:** `phase 8 — explainable risk engine`
